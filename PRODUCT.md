# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Sports fans checking an upcoming or completed fixture on a phone and practicing predictions with fictional credits.

## Product Purpose

Matchday Plan combines live fixture data, source-backed matchup context, a fictional-credit wager simulator, and personal results so a fan can make a call and see how it ages.

## Positioning

The product connects each simulated pick to the same real fixture data and evidence shown on the matchup page. It is practice and record keeping, not a sportsbook.

## Operating Context

People scan the next games, filter by sport, open a matchup, compare recent form and standings, and optionally place a fictional-credit pick. Most of this flow happens quickly on a phone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Soccer and baseball fixtures come from external providers and may have partial context.
- Browsing works without an account; balances, wager placement, history, statistics, and profile actions require configured authentication.
- Credits are fictional, cannot be bought, transferred, or withdrawn, and must never be presented as real money.
- Finished, postponed, and cancelled games cannot accept new wagers.
- Missing lineups, head-to-head records, crests, or statistics must render honestly rather than be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Matchday Plan.
- Mark: MP.
- The supplied mobile redesign image is the visual authority for the home and matchup surfaces.

## Evidence on Hand

- Provider-backed schedules, results, standings, recent form, probable pitchers, and selected advanced statistics.
- Local wager ledger and personal record data when authentication is configured.
- No guaranteed lineup feed for every fixture.

## Product Principles

- Put the next useful action in the first viewport.
- Keep real data and fictional credits visibly distinct.
- Prefer compact, glanceable sports information over explanatory chrome.
- Show unavailable states instead of fabricated facts.
- Preserve keyboard, reduced-motion, zoom, and mobile-safe-area support.
