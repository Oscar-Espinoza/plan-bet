# Team crests

Bundled originals for the four tracked clubs, retrieved September 12, 2026:

- Real Madrid: https://crests.football-data.org/86.svg
- FC Barcelona: https://crests.football-data.org/81.svg
- New York Yankees: https://www.mlbstatic.com/team-logos/147.svg
- Boston Red Sox: https://www.mlbstatic.com/team-logos/111.svg

Team marks belong to their respective clubs. Preserve their original colors and proportions.

Opponent crests come from the football-data match response or MLB's team-ID asset URL. Demo schedules specify their opponent assets explicitly. Older cached games without opponent crests continue showing team names; a provider refresh adds the available crests. Unknown or failed images are omitted, and the full team name remains visible.
